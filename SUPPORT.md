# Support Quantaflex

Quantaflex is independently developed professional software.

Commercial product sales fund the core business.

Community sponsorship helps accelerate development, testing, documentation, research, and future capabilities.

## Sponsorship Opens

**15 October 2026**

## Sponsorship Levels

### Quantaflex Supporter
**£5 / €6 per month**

Includes access to the Quantaflex desktop application while sponsorship remains active, plus community access, development updates, release notes, and supporter recognition.

### Quantaflex Builder
**£10 / €12 per month**

Includes Supporter benefits plus:

- early access to new features
- beta builds
- roadmap previews
- community voting on development priorities

### Quantaflex Pioneer
**£20 / €24 per month**

Includes Builder benefits plus:

- experimental builds
- prototype features
- deeper technical development updates
- selected benchmarks
- optional Pioneer acknowledgement

### Quantaflex Sponsor
**£50 / €60 per month**

Includes Pioneer benefits plus:

- periodic roadmap briefings
- priority consideration for workflow and compatibility requests
- sponsor acknowledgement

## Permanent Licence

Quantaflex will also be available as a permanent commercial licence through the official Shopify store.

Users can choose between:

- purchasing Quantaflex outright
- supporting development through monthly sponsorship

## Important

Sponsorship does not grant access to:

- Quantaflex source code
- private development repositories
- proprietary codec implementation
- confidential algorithms
- internal research material
- unreleased intellectual property

Sponsor benefits relate to product access, previews, community participation, and development involvement.

## Founding Supporters

We plan to recognise early supporters joining during the launch period with Founding Supporter status.

Further details will be published before sponsorship opens.
